The Work

Value cases built for scrutiny.

We work with teams selling operationally complex products and services. Starting with a live opportunity, we translate customer inputs, operational realities, and credible assumptions into one coherent case for change.

The work may include an ROI model, executive narrative, internal presentation, and an evidence appendix. The output is not collateral — it is a decision tool.

Why It Matters

A business case is not a pitch deck.

Forrester found that 97% of technology decision-makers use business cases to justify purchases. Gartner reports that 74% of B2B buying teams experience unhealthy conflict while making a decision. The job is not to make a bigger claim. It is to make the right claim supportable.
