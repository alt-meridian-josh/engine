The Problem

The product is not always the problem

A complex deal can stall even when the customer likes the solution.

The customer champion still needs to explain:

  • What is the current problem costing us?
  • Why does this investment matter now?
  • Which benefits are credible enough to include?
  • What will finance, procurement, or leadership challenge?
  • When could the investment pay back?

I turn those questions into a clear business case.

What You Get

A value case built for real internal review

  • A view of the customer's current cost, risk, or missed opportunity
  • The few value drivers that matter most
  • ROI and payback calculations
  • Customer inputs, benchmarks, assumptions, and source notes
  • A concise business case the customer champion can take to finance
  • Seller guidance for the next executive, finance, or procurement conversation

A defensible ROI case makes the logic visible. It distinguishes customer facts from benchmarks, labels assumptions, avoids double-counting, and separates conservative value from upside.
