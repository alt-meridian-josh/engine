The Problem

The product isn't always the problem

Even when the buyer likes the solution, their champion still has to answer:

  • What is the current problem costing us?
  • Why does this investment matter now?
  • Which benefits are credible enough to include?
  • What will finance, procurement, or leadership challenge?
  • When could the investment pay back?

Alt Meridian turns those questions into a defensible business case.
