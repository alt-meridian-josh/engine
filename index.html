About

Josh Willis

I’m a value engineer. I build ROI models and business cases for enterprise technology sales, and spend most of my time with sales teams, customer champions, and the finance people who have to say yes.

Before that I worked in geospatial analytics and insurance technology, including at Cape Analytics. I started in procurement at Halliburton with a geology degree, and later earned a master’s in GIS from Texas A&M.

I started Alt Meridian to do this work for companies that don’t have a value team, and to write about how value works where people can see it.
